# 3-Minute Demonstration Video Script: CareBridge AI

**Title:** CareBridge AI – Intelligent Healthcare Access and Assistance Platform  
**Target Duration:** 3 minutes (180 seconds)  
**Persona:** Sushila Devi, 58 years old, Gram Madhuban, Mathura (Uttar Pradesh)  
**Demo Account:** `patient@carebridge.org` / `asha@carebridge.org` / `doctor@carebridge.org`

---

## Scene 1: The Rural Healthcare Challenge (0:00 – 0:20)
* **Visual:** Opening title card: *CareBridge AI — Building for Bharat*. Show persona card for Sushila Devi (58 years, rural Mathura).
* **Voiceover (Narration):**
  > "Meet Sushila Devi, a 58-year-old grandmother from a village near Mathura. When she develops severe chest tightness and breathlessness, her local primary clinic has no cardiologist. Her journey to the district hospital means traveling 40 kilometers into crowded, confusing corridors, waiting hours without information, and fearing whether her dispensed medicines are genuine."

---

## Scene 2: Introducing CareBridge AI (0:20 – 0:35)
* **Visual:** Transition to CareBridge AI landing dashboard. Show the 3 interconnected pillars: **CareConnect**, **CareNav**, and **MediTrust**.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "CareBridge AI unites three critical healthcare pillars into one digital ecosystem built specifically for Bharat: rural specialist diagnostic support, intelligent hospital navigation, and verified medicine traceability."

---

## Scene 3: Module 1 — CareConnect Tele-Triage & Referral (0:35 – 1:15)
* **Visual:** Log in as ASHA worker Pooja Verma (`asha@carebridge.org`). Open "Submit Clinical Case", enter vitals for Sushila Devi (BP 165/102, SpO2 92.5%, chest tightness).
* **Action:** Click "Submit Case & Evaluate Triage". Show explainable triage result: **Urgent Review (82% risk score)** with contributing factors and cardiologist recommendation.
* **Action:** Fast switch to Dr. Rajesh Sharma (`doctor@carebridge.org`). Specialist opens referral, inputs clinical notes, and accepts referral to AIIMS District Hub.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "At the village clinic, ASHA worker Pooja Verma inputs Sushila's vitals. CareBridge's explainable triage engine immediately flags hypertensive hypoxemia and alerts Dr. Rajesh Sharma, a cardiologist at the district hospital. Within minutes, Dr. Sharma reviews the tele-case and schedules an expedited consultation."

---

## Scene 4: Module 2 — CareNav Hospital Navigation & Digital Queue (1:15 – 1:55)
* **Visual:** Switch to Patient Dashboard (`patient@carebridge.org`). Show active digital queue token **CARDIO-014** and estimated waiting time.
* **Action:** Click "Hospital Map". Select source: *Main Entrance Gate A*, destination: *Cardiology Suite 201 (Floor 2)*. Click "Find Shortest Route".
* **Action:** Show interactive Dijkstra SVG path highlighting elevator transitions. Click "Listen / बोलकर सुनाएं" to trigger Hindi voice guidance.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "Arriving at the district hospital, Sushila receives her digital queue token on her phone, with an estimated wait of just 4 minutes. With our accessible indoor hospital navigator, she receives turn-by-turn guidance and Hindi voice assistance directing her straight to Cardiology Suite 201 on the second floor."

---

## Scene 5: Module 3 — MediTrust Medicine Verification (1:55 – 2:25)
* **Visual:** Navigate to "Verify Medicine".
* **Action:** Click "Load Valid Demo QR". Show instant authentic status: Amoxicillin-Clavulanate, batch details, CDSCO Jan Aushadhi registration, and cryptographic SHA-256 supply chain audit trail.
* **Action:** Click "Load Expired Demo QR" and "Load Suspicious Demo" to show red expired alert and anomaly isolation flags.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "After her consultation, Sushila collects her prescribed medicines from the Jan Aushadhi pharmacy. Scanning the package QR code with MediTrust verifies its batch authenticity, confirms it is in-date, and proves an unbroken supply chain from manufacturer to dispensary."

---

## Scene 6: Unified Healthcare Journey Summary (2:25 – 2:45)
* **Visual:** Return to Patient Dashboard. Show the complete 7-step visual journey timeline showing completed steps from PHC intake to medicine dispensing.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "All 7 steps of Sushila Devi's healthcare journey are connected in one unified record — from village clinic to specialist consultation, diagnostic labs, and authentic medication."

---

## Scene 7: Impact & Closing (2:45 – 3:00)
* **Visual:** Show Admin Dashboard analytics (waiting time reduction, patient load, verification metrics). Display closing slogan: *CareBridge AI — Intelligent, Accessible Healthcare for All of Bharat*.
* **Voiceover:**
  > "CareBridge AI reduces patient anxiety, eliminates unnecessary travel, and builds trust in rural healthcare. Intelligent, accessible, and built for Bharat."
